Date - Date on which the sample was collected

Quarter:	
Winter (1)
Spring (2)
Summer (3)
Autumn (4)

Lat_deg	- Latitude of the collection station in degrees

Depthm - Depth at which the sample was collected (m)

Zone:	
Depth < 200m (Photic)
200m < Depth < 1000m (Dysphotic)
1000m < Depth (Aphotic)

T_degC - Sample temperature in Celsius
